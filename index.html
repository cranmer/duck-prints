Signed & numbered waterfowl stamp prints

Duck Stamp Prints, 1981–1991

Twenty-one framed prints, each matted with the stamp it was painted for. They make two unbroken runs: ten Federal prints from 1981-82 through 1990-91, and the first ten Arkansas state prints from 1981-82 through 1990-91. Every Arkansas print is number 105 of a 600-print special series. A 1984 reissue of Ding Darling's 1934 design for the first Federal stamp completes the set.

  • 10 Federal
  • 10 Arkansas
  • 1 commemorative
  • Arkansas special series 105/600
